# Amazon Sales Analysis using Power BI
## Dashboard Preview

![Executive Dashboard](Dashboard%20Images/Executive%20Dashboard.png)
## Project Overview

This project analyzes Amazon product data to uncover insights related to product performance, customer engagement, pricing strategy, and discount effectiveness. The dashboard combines descriptive, diagnostic, and advanced analytics techniques to help businesses identify high-performing products, underperforming products, and opportunities for optimization.
The solution was developed using Power BI, Power Query, and DAX to create an interactive and executive-level business intelligence dashboard.

## Objectives

* Analyze product ratings and customer engagement.
* Identify top-performing and underperforming products.
* Evaluate the impact of discounts on customer ratings.
* Develop a product performance scoring model.
* Rank products dynamically based on business metrics.
* Detect review-rating mismatches and pricing inefficiencies.
* Build an executive storytelling dashboard for decision-making.

## Dataset Information

* Total Products: **1,351**
* Total Reviews: **1,194**
* Average Product Rating: **4.10 / 5**
* Average Discount Offered: **48%**

## Key Insights

* Over 90% of products received ratings between 4 and 5 stars, indicating high customer satisfaction.
* The Electronics category generated the highest estimated revenue and customer engagement.
* Higher discounts did not consistently result in higher customer ratings.
* Several products with heavy discounts continued to underperform, indicating pricing inefficiencies.
* Dynamic ranking identified top-performing products suitable for promotional campaigns and inventory prioritization.


## Business Recommendations

* Prioritize inventory and marketing efforts for high-performing product categories.
* Optimize pricing strategies rather than relying solely on large discounts.
* Investigate underperforming products using customer feedback and ratings.
* Promote top-ranked products through featured listings and bundled offers.
* Encourage verified customer reviews to improve customer trust and engagement.
